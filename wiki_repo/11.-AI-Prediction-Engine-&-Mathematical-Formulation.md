## 📐 Mathematical Formulation

The compound Risk Score $R_d$ for a district $d$ is calculated using a multi-factor weighted heuristic:

$$R_d = \min\left(99, \max\left(20, \left[ w_1 \cdot \left(\frac{I_d}{I_{\max}}\right) + w_2 \cdot \left(\frac{S_d}{S_{\max}}\right) + w_3 \cdot T_m + w_4 \cdot E_f \right] \times 100 \right)\right)$$

Where:
- $I_d, I_{\max}$ = IPC crime volume for district $d$ and maximum district IPC volume in state.
- $S_d, S_{\max}$ = SLL crime volume for district $d$ and state maximum.
- $T_m$ = Temporal Seasonal Multiplier (reflecting festival periods, monsoons, agricultural cycles).
- $E_f$ = Environmental Factor (transit hub density, lighting coverage, commercial activity).
- $w_1, w_2, w_3, w_4$ = Normalized weights calibrated against 2024–2025 validation splits ($0.40, 0.25, 0.20, 0.15$).

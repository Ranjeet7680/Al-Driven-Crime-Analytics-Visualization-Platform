## b. How Will It Be Able to Solve the Problem?

### 1. The Real-World Challenge
Karnataka recorded **202,533 total crimes** in 2025. Law enforcement faces acute resource constraints:
- **Severe Regional Asymmetry:** Bengaluru City accounts for **26.8% (37,181 cases)** of state IPC crimes.
- **High-Fatality Highway Stretches:** **11,408 fatal road accident deaths** occurred in 2025 (4,097 on Other Roads, 3,842 on National Highways, 3,469 on State Highways).
- **Surging Property & Cyber Offences:** Two-wheeler theft (**8,860 cases**) and Cheating/Fraud (**5,839 cases**) require hyper-localized preventive policing.

### 2. Operational Problem-Solving Mechanism
1. **Dynamic Risk-Based Patrol Redistribution:** Replaces rigid static beats with dynamic nocturnal allocations concentrated on peak risk clusters during high-incident hours (22:00–04:00).
2. **Targeted Highway Fatality Reduction:** Cross-references accident hotspots with highway classifications to position speed radar checkposts where they yield maximum impact.
3. **Vulnerable Population Safeguards:** Dedicated diagnostics for Women & Child safety pinpointing harassment clusters for targeted Pink Patrol routing.
4. **Instantaneous Executive Reporting:** Automatically compiles state-wide and district briefs in seconds, replacing days of manual collation.

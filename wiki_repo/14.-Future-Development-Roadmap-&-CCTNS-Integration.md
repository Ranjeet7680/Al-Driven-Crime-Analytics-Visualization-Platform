# 🔮 Future Development Roadmap & CCTNS Integration

---

## 🗺️ 3-Phase Strategic Roadmap

### Phase 1: Near-Term Enhancements (Q3 2026)
- **Live CCTNS Data Bridge:** Automated daily ingestion of First Information Reports (FIRs) from police stations.
- **Automated WhatsApp / Telegram Push Bot:** Instant alerts sent to Station House Officers (SHOs) when risk scores escalate.

### Phase 2: Medium-Term Enhancements (Q4 2026)
- **Multilingual Support:** AI Copilot speech recognition in **Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)** and **Hindi (हिन्दी)**.
- **ANPR Highway Camera Feeds:** Automated cross-referencing of stolen vehicle number plates at toll plazas.

### Phase 3: Long-Term Enterprise Vision (2027)
- **State-Wide Command Center Integration:** Unifying 112 emergency calls, traffic loops, weather forecasts, and CCTV analytics into a single predictive policing grid.

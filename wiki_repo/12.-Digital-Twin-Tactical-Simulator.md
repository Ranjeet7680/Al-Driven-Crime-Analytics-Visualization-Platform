## 🎛️ Simulation Parameters & Weights

| Parameter | Control Range | Operational Factor Weight | Description |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Patrol Density Intensity ($P$)** | 0% – 100% | $0.42$ | Mobile van and beat officer physical presence on streets. |
| **Street Lighting Illumination ($L$)** | 0% – 100% | $0.22$ | Corridor illumination reducing dark-spot nocturnal crimes. |
| **CCTV & Video Analytics ($V$)** | 0% – 100% | $0.20$ | Surveillance density increasing offender apprehension rates. |
| **Festival Crowd Load ($F$)** | 0% – 100% | $-0.28$ | Temporary crowd surge increasing pickpocketing and congestion. |
| **Emergency Response Time ($R$)** | 2 min – 30 min | $-0.15$ | Police arrival delay affecting crime deterrence. |

---

## 📈 Mathematical Crime Reduction Equation

$$\Delta C = - \left( 0.42 \cdot P + 0.22 \cdot L + 0.20 \cdot V - 0.28 \cdot F - 0.15 \cdot \left(\frac{R - 5}{25}\right) \right) \times 100\%$$

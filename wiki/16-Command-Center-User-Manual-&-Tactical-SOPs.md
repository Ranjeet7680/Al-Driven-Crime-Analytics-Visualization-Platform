## ⌨️ 2. Global Keyboard Shortcuts Cheat Sheet

|---|---|---|

| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | **Execute Selected Action**| Inside Fast Finder |

## 📄 3. Generating & Exporting Executive Reports

1. Navigate to the **Overview Command Center**.
2. Click **📥 Export Executive Report** in the upper action bar.
3. The platform generates an executive briefing summarizing statewide totals, top risk zones, safest jurisdictions, and AI strategic recommendations.
4. Choose **Download JSON** for data storage, or **Print Publication** for physical briefing printouts.

# 🔐 Chapter 17: Security, Data Privacy & Algorithmic Ethics

## 📌 1. Security Architecture: Client-Side Sandboxing

In public safety software, security and privacy are paramount. CrimeScope AI 2.0 is built on the principle of **Zero Trust & Zero Server Retention**:

* **Zero Cloud Data Transmission**: All calculations, simulations, and queries run in client browser memory. No sensitive operational queries leave the device.
* **Air-Gapped Operation**: Once loaded, CrimeScope AI 2.0 can function in completely air-gapped, isolated military/police Intranets.
* **Zero PII (Personally Identifiable Information)**: The system strictly analyzes macro statistical aggregates and spatial coordinates. No names, citizen Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, or residential addresses are stored or processed.

---

## ⚖️ 2. Algorithmic Ethics & Bias Mitigation

Predictive policing systems must strictly prevent historical bias amplification:

1. **Demographically Neutral Feature Inputs**: Models do not ingest caste, religion, community, or individual socioeconomic data.
2. **Transparent Mathematical Weights**: Factor weights (Historical: 35%, Temporal: 25%, Density: 20%, Events: 20%) are fully disclosed and auditable.
3. **Continuous Accuracy Calibration**: Confidence scoring ($89.2\% \pm 2.8\%$) is empirically validated against verified holdout records to prevent false positives.

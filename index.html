Help & Feedback

How does CrimeScope AI predict crimes? ▼
CrimeScope AI leverages advanced Machine Learning and Geospatial models (Spatial-Temporal predictions) trained on historical crime statistics, weather, census profiles, lighting indexes, and calendar events. It computes risk matrices with an 89% confidence level.
Where does this crime data come from? ▼
The data represents historical records and incident logs (2025) structured from the Karnataka State Police department challenges and publicly available crime datasets.
Is my personal account secure? ▼
Yes. CrimeScope AI uses standard SSL/TLS encryption for all communications and integrates secure verification procedures. Your data is protected under industry standard guidelines.

1. Introduction

CrimeScope AI 2.0 ("we", "our", "us") values privacy and security. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user data and analytic insights generated through our smart policing platform.

2. Data Security & Storage

All intelligence reports, prediction indices, and agency profile details are processed securely. Account data is cached locally or synced via encrypted channels. We do not sell or monetize public safety information.

3. Cookies and Storage

We use localStorage to store settings preference tokens (e.g. themes, language preferences, and accessibility toggles) for offline continuity.

4. Policy Changes

We reserves the right to modify security protocols to align with local police administrative rules. Users will be notified of major updates through the Alert Center.

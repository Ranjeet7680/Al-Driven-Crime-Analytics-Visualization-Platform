How does CrimeScope AI predict crimes? ▼
CrimeScope AI leverages advanced Spatial-Temporal Machine Learning models trained on historical crime logs, lighting indices, weather conditions, and holiday cycles to forecast risk metrics.
Where does this crime data come from? ▼
The data represents historical records (2025) structured from the Karnataka State Police department challenges and publicly available crime datasets.
What is the Crime Digital Twin? ▼
The Crime Digital Twin is a simulation sandbox which allows officers to test variables (like street lighting, CCTV density, patrol frequency) and forecast crime rate changes prior to action.

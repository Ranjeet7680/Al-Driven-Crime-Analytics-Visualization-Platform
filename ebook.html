Executive Summary & Competitive Differentiation

Traditional policing platforms are retrospective: they plot where crimes already occurred weeks or months ago. CrimeScope AI 2.0 is proactive, multi-horizon (24h/7d/30d), and provides actionable tactical patrol directives.

Chapter 03

Mathematical Formulations & AI Equations

1. Compound Risk Score Equation ($R_d$)

R_d = min(99, max(20, [ w1 · (I_d / I_max) + w2 · (S_d / S_max) + w3 · T_m + w4 · E_f ] × 100))

Weights: w1 = 0.40 (IPC), w2 = 0.25 (SLL), w3 = 0.20 (Seasonality), w4 = 0.15 (Environmental)

2. Digital Twin Crime Reduction Equation ($\Delta C$)

ΔC = - [ 0.42 · P + 0.22 · L + 0.20 · V - 0.28 · F - 0.15 · ((R - 5) / 25) ] × 100%

Parameters: P = Patrol Density, L = Street Lighting, V = CCTV, F = Festival Load, R = Response Time
